# Virgin Queen: Wars of Religion 1559-1598 — Complete Rules Extraction
## (VQ 2024 Living Rules, by Ed Beach, GMT Games)

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION (Section 1)

- **Game**: Virgin Queen — military, political, and religious conflicts in Europe during the reigns of Elizabeth I and Philip II
- **Players**: 2–6 players
- **Major Powers (6)**: Ottoman, Spain, England, France, Holy Roman Empire, Protestant
- **Minor Powers (5)**: Ireland, Papacy, Portugal, Scotland, Venice
- **Turns**: Up to 7 turns; each turn covers 5–6 years
- **Impulse Order** (used throughout for resolving ties and sequencing): 1. Ottoman, 2. Spain, 3. England, 4. France, 5. Holy Roman, 6. Protestant

---

## 2. GAME MAP (Section 2)

### 2.1 Spaces
- **Europe Map**: 22"×34" map with cities/towns as spaces connected by lines
- **Fortified spaces** (walled cities):
  - **Keys** (square symbol): Most valuable; grant VP and cards. Double-square border = capital.
  - **Fortresses** (8-pointed circle): Strong defenses, no VP/cards. Can be built during game.
- **Unfortified spaces** (circle): Can be controlled without siege. Forces from two at-war powers in same unfortified space must fight a field battle.
- **Home spaces**: Color-coded by power. Gray = independent.
- **Ports**: Circular symbol with anchor; provide access to sea zones. One-zone or two-zone ports.
- **Split home spaces**: 19 spaces color-coded with two home powers (top/bottom). Bottom power controls initially.
- **Passes**: Dashed-line connections (4 Pyrenees, 6 Alps, 7 Balkans). Cost 2 CP to cross. Block spring deployment, prevent interception across, inhibit adjacent control/unrest removal. Units may retreat or avoid battle over a pass.
- **Inland waterways**: Brielle↔Rotterdam and Flushing↔Antwerp (squiggly blue lines). Only usable by power controlling the normal port (Flushing or Brielle) or its ally. Always connected for Conversion/Rebellion purposes.

### 2.2 Political Control
- Default: each space controlled by its home power
- Control markers placed when control changes to a non-home power
- **Controlled spaces** for a power: own home spaces not fallen + seized independent/enemy spaces + allied minor power home spaces
- **Friendly**: Controlled by active power or ally
- **Enemy**: Controlled by power at war with active power
- **Independent**: Not controlled by any major/minor power; never friendly or enemy

### 2.3 Religious Influence
- **Four religious struggle areas**: Netherlands (orange), France (dark blue), England (red), Scotland (light blue)
- Each space tracked as Catholic or Protestant
- **Catholic**: Solid-colored control marker side (or no marker on solid space)
- **Protestant**: Colored-border-with-white-center side

### 2.4 Control Markers
- **Square markers**: For keys. Must be on map or power card at all times.
- **Hexagonal markers**: For fortresses and unfortified spaces. No limit per power.

### 2.5 Europe Map Elements
- **Sea zones (13 total)**:
  - 8 Mediterranean (blue text): Barbary Coast, Tyrrhenian Sea, Adriatic Sea, Gulf of Lyon, Ionian Sea, Aegean Sea, North African Coast, Eastern Mediterranean
  - 5 Atlantic (black text): Irish Sea, North Sea, English Channel, Bay of Biscay, Atlantic Ocean
  - Galleons penalized intercepting/avoiding in Mediterranean zones
  - Galleys less effective in Atlantic zones
  - Galleons must be built in ports bordering Atlantic zones
- **Non-connected sea zones**: Ionian/Tyrrhenian (two-zone port: Messina); Barbary Coast/Atlantic Ocean (two-zone port: Gibraltar)

### 2.6 World Map Elements
- **10 Ocean zones**: 8 around Americas, Indian Ocean, South Pacific
- **Settlements**: Triangular symbols; sea captains/patrols cannot enter them
- **Trade winds**: Light blue arrows between ocean zones. Moving against trade wind requires Navigation Check.
- **Hazardous passages**: Dangerous connections between ocean zones. Always require Navigation roll regardless of direction.
- Units may never move directly between World Map and Europe Map.

## 3. POWERS AND RULERS (Section 3)

### 3.1 Players
- 2–6 human players. All 6 major powers always in play.
- In <6 player games: Ottoman and Holy Roman alternate between inactive/active states.

### 3.2 Powers
- **6 Major Powers**: Ottoman, Spain, England, France, Holy Roman, Protestant
- **5 Minor Powers**: Ireland, Papacy, Portugal, Scotland, Venice
- "Power" without qualifier = major power

### 3.3 Rulers
- Each major power has a ruler with two attributes:
  - **Administrative Rating**: Number of cards that can be saved turn-to-turn
  - **Card Bonus**: Extra cards dealt (usually +1 card)
- **Suleiman's special card bonus**: (a) Retain both Home cards; (b) Receive Selim II card at start of Turn 2 if still in deck
- Rulers change via Mandatory Events for Ottoman, France, Holy Roman
- Last rulers (never replaced by Mandatory Event): Philip II (Spain), Elizabeth I (England), Henry III (France), Rudolf II (Holy Roman) — but can die via Marriage/Assassination
- Henry III and Protestant army leaders also function as army leaders (separate from ruler role)

## 4. POWER CARDS (Section 4)

- Each major power has a power card showing:
  - **List of Actions** (upper-left): Available actions with CP costs, color-coded by type
  - **Initial Ruler Card** (upper-right): Ruler attributes
  - **Bonus VP Box** (lower-right): Holds earned bonus VP markers
  - **Lower-left**: Card draw calculation, base VP calculation, power-specific tracks/boxes

## 5. MILITARY UNITS (Section 5)

**Counter limits are absolute — no more may be created beyond provided pieces.**

### 5.1 Leaders
- **Army Leaders** (plastic stands or flat counters):
  - **Battle Rating** (top number): Adds dice in combat; aids intercept/avoid battle
  - **Command Rating** (bottom, yellow box): Max land units the leader can control
- **Formation rules**:
  - No leaders: max 4 units
  - 1 leader: command rating of that leader
  - 2+ leaders: sum of highest two command ratings
  - Leaders never count toward formation size
  - Formations may not mix units from two major powers (except major + allied minor)
- **Naval Leaders**: Battle Rating on top. Ottoman naval leaders (Dragut, Uluch Ali) also have Piracy Rating.
- **Default Leaders**: If last army leader of a power removed, place default leader on Turn Track. Enters if no newly arriving standard leader exists. Captured leaders count as "in play."

### 5.2 Sea Captains
- Two-sided flat counters (Full Strength / Damaged)
- **Battle Rating**: Top left
- **Piracy Rating**: Middle left
- **Navigation Rating**: After "N" — die roll modifier for treacherous voyages
- De Sores and Le Clerc: Available to either French or Protestant (whoever builds expedition first)
- English sea captains with Battle Rating 1 may serve as naval leaders on Europe Map

### 5.3 Land Units
- Circular pieces in denominations (1, 2, 4, 6)
- **Regulars**: Multi-colored symbol, solid stripe. All powers have regulars.
- **Mercenaries**: Solid black symbol, pale background. All major powers except Ottoman. Cheaper but may desert.
- **Cavalry**: Ottoman only. Aid intercept/avoid battle, ineffective during siege.
- **Knights of St. John**: Spanish regular for all purposes. Cannot be built with Build Regular; may never move from current space.

### 5.4 Naval Units
- Rectangular pieces, each = 1 group of vessels. Number = combat dice AND hits to eliminate.
- **10 powers** receive naval units:
  - Ottoman: 9 (Galley/Corsair)
  - Spain: 7 (Galley/Galleon)
  - England: 6 (Galleon/Race-built Galleon)
  - France: 4 (Galley/Galleon)
  - Holy Roman: 1 (Galley)
  - Dutch: 2 (Galleon)
  - Huguenot: 2 (Galleon)
  - Papacy: 1 (Galley)
  - Portugal: 2 (Galleon)
  - Venice: 4 (Galley)
- **Galley**: Less effective in Atlantic. Low-sided, white sails.
- **Corsair**: Ottoman only. Can pirate without war. Low-sided, black sails.
- **Galleon**: Equal across map, penalized intercepting/avoiding in Mediterranean. High-sided, white sails.
- **Race-built Galleon**: England only. Like galleon but takes less damage in Atlantic.
- **Patrol**: Spain only. Built in ocean zones. Protects settlements from piracy.
- **Spanish Armada**: Special unit, see 23.2.

## 6. CARDS AND TREASURES (Section 6)

### 6.1 Card Types (112 cards + 30 treasures)

1. **Home Cards**: 1–2 per power per turn. Must discard to 1 during Spring Deployment (exception: Ottoman under Suleiman keeps both). Cannot be taken by random draw, espionage, or piracy. Placed on power card after use (not discard pile).

2. **Mandatory Event Cards** (red name, "Mandatory"): Must be played during Action Phase of turn drawn. Event resolves first, then 2 CP for actions. Cannot be held turn-to-turn. Cannot pass with one in hand. Cannot be used for DOW. Cannot be retrieved from discard.

3. **Response Cards** (blue name, "Response"): Playable as event during ANY player's impulse. Interrupts impulse/battle/event. Can also be played for CP during own impulse.

4. **Combat Cards** (black name, "Combat"): Playable as event only during field battle, assault, or naval combat involving owner's units. Played just before resolution. Can also be played for CP.

5. **Event Cards** (black name): Played as events during owning player's impulse. Some have conditions. Cards with "OR" give player choice of effects.

### 6.2 The Deck
- Single shared deck, reshuffled each turn
- **Adding cards**: 39 cards have turn numbers in upper-right. Added at start of specified turn's Card Draw Phase.
- **Dealing cards**: Number determined by square control markers on power card. Last uncovered square in "Cards and VP Per Key" shows deal count. Unrest markers on power card further reduce deal. Minimum: 1 base card even if all covered. Then add ruler card bonus, then +1/−1 card markers.
- **Hand** = dealt cards + Home card(s) + any held from previous turn
- **Discard pile**: Shared by all players. Cards go here unless "Remove from deck if played as event" (and played as event). Reincorporated into deck each turn.
- **Retrieval restrictions** (The Virgin Queen card, Gouvernante de France, Papal Bull): Cannot retrieve Mandatory Events. Cannot retrieve a card you played as Event/Response/Combat earlier this turn.

### 6.3 Treasures
- 30 treasure counters, kept face-down in pool
- **Spanish treasures**: 4 boxes filled each turn (5 if Spain controls Philippines). Spanish player may view.
- **Portuguese treasures**: 3 boxes filled. No one may view until captured/returned.
- **Receiving treasures**: Spain via Treasure Fleet event/Winter Phase. Others via colony rolls/expedition loot.
- **Mixed hand rule** (random draws from hand with both cards and treasures):
  - More cards than treasures → draw from cards
  - More treasures or equal → draw from treasures

## 7. SEQUENCE OF PLAY (Section 7)

**7 turns maximum. Each turn = 8 phases:**

| # | Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Card Draw Phase** | Add personages, fortresses, Jesuits, new cards; shuffle deck; deal cards; refresh treasures; colony rolls |
| 2 | **Diplomacy Phase** | Negotiations → Ransom → Declarations of War |
| 3 | **Spring Deployment Phase** | Place leaders, choose Home card, move 1 formation from capital; special power-specific steps |
| 4 | **Action Phase** | Powers take impulses in Impulse Order until all pass consecutively |
| 5 | **Winter Phase** | Morisco unrest, discard treasures, remove loans, return units home, end alliances, replacements, piracy reset, espionage reset, diplomacy reset, Catholic League check |
| 6 | **Marriage Resolution Phase** | Resolve marriages, Virgin Queen VP, aging |
| 7 | **Patronage Phase** | Evaluate artists, evaluate scientists |
| 8 | **Victory Determination Phase** | Paris/Central Europe VP; check for Standard/Domination/Time Limit victory |

## 8. CARD DRAW PHASE (Section 8)

### 8.1 Add New Personages
- Turn 2+: Add army/naval leaders, sea captains, royals, artists, scientists per Turn Track
- **Uluch Ali** replaces Dragut if killed by Dragut Falls! event
- **Default leaders**: Enter if no standard army leader arriving
- **Duke of Guise**: Only leader that enters during Winter Phase (Catholic League formation)
- **Naval units**: Eliminated units returned to force pool here
- Other personages placed in appropriate areas (sea captains → force pool; royals → chart; artists/scientists → patronage chart)

### 8.2 Add Fortresses and Jesuits (Spain only)
- **Fortresses**: 1 marker on power card (2 if Coastal Fortification science bonus)
- **Jesuits**: 1 marker on power card (2 if English College, Douai event played). Max 5 total Jesuits on map + power card.

### 8.3 Add New Cards / Deal Cards
- See Section 6.2

### 8.4 Refresh Treasures / Colony Rolls
- Return discarded treasures to pool. Deal Spanish/Portuguese treasures.
- **Colony rolls**: Power with colony rolls 2d6 per colony, consult Colony Table. +1 to roll if Plantation science bonus.
  - **Eliminated**: Colony removed; expeditions move to adjacent ocean zone
  - **NE**: No effect
  - **Treasure**: Draw 1 treasure, keep colony
  - **Treasure + Virginia Dare VP**: Draw 1 treasure, keep colony, +1 VP

## 9. DIPLOMACY (Section 9)

### 9.1 Negotiations
- First segment: Secret negotiations (only time private talks allowed)
- **Binding changes to game state** (the only enforceable agreements):
  - End a war (Exception: Spain-England can NEVER agree to peace)
  - Form 1-turn alliance (not if at war unless ending that war)
  - Loan naval units/leaders to ally (1 turn; one direction only)
  - Return captured army leader (to capital or friendly home key)
  - Yield political control of spaces (with restrictions on which spaces)
  - Give up to 2 random cards to another power (one direction per turn)
  - Give up to 2 random treasures (one direction per turn)
  - Give up to 4 mercenaries (all except Ottoman can give; Ottoman and Holy Roman cannot receive)
  - Arrange marriage of royals
- **Non-binding agreements**: Anything that doesn't change current game state. No in-game repercussion for breaking.
- **Announcement order**: Impulse Order. Earlier power proposes; later power confirms.
- Conditional announcements prohibited.
- Suggested time limits: 10 min face-to-face, 48 hours email.

### 9.2 Alliances
- One turn only; announced at end of Negotiation Segment
- **Restrictions**:
  - Not if at war (unless ending war simultaneously)
  - Spain & Protestant: NEVER
  - France: May ally Spain OR Protestant, not both same turn
  - Spain & England: Not if DOW cost between them ≤ 3 CP
- **Effects**:
  - Spaces of each power become friendly to both
  - Naval units may be loaned (one direction, with Loaned markers)
  - Combined defense if stack of two allied powers attacked
  - Alliances do NOT allow combined movement/siege/assault/intercept/avoid-battle
  - Alliances do NOT create new wars with third parties

### 9.3 Ransom of Leaders
- Pay ransom (1 random card draw from your hand) to regain captured army leader
- No requirement to ransom; can leave captive indefinitely

### 9.4 Declarations of War
**DOW Procedure:**
1. DOW on Major Powers: Add "At War" marker
2. DOW on Minor Powers: Add "At War" marker; triggers diplomatic status resolution; declaring power loses influence with that minor permanently
3. Pay DOW Costs: CP cost from Diplomatic Status Display cross-reference. Play cards/treasures. Home cards and Mandatory Events cannot pay for DOW.
4. Intervention on Behalf of Minor: Winner of diplomatic status check chooses Intervention (ally the minor, go to war with declaring power) or Isolation (pass; second-place power gets option)

**Restrictions (all times):**
- Cannot DOW on "N/A" or "Possible Ally" powers
- Cannot DOW minor allied to major (must DOW the major)
- Cannot DOW current ally
- Cannot DOW Portugal before Death of King Sebastian played
- Cannot DOW independent fortified spaces (Metz/Florence) — just besiege

**Restrictions (Diplomacy Phase only):**
- Cannot DOW on power you made peace with this phase
- Cannot DOW on power you allied with this phase

**DOW during Action Phase triggers:**
- Ottoman Tribute / Holy Roman Intercession events
- Rebellion
- Spanish troops entering Scotland/Ireland
- English troops entering Netherlands (unless allied with Spain)
- Minor power activation

### 9.5 War Between England and Spain
- **DOW cost starts at 5 CP**, reduced by:
  - Papal Bull (Elizabeth excommunicated): −2 CP
  - Scottish Lords Rebel (Mary captured): −2 CP
  - England executes Mary Queen of Scots: −3 CP
  - **Minimum cost: 1 CP**
- **Immediate war triggers** (listed on Diplomatic Status Display)
- Spanish units may enter Ireland/Scotland only when DOW cost ≤ 3 CP
- **Once at war, England and Spain may NEVER agree to peace**

## 10. SPRING DEPLOYMENT (Section 10)

Performed in Impulse Order. Each power completes all steps before next.

1. **Treasure Fleet Escorts** (Spain): Move galleons to/from Treasure Fleet Escorts box (max 2 galleons, Spanish only)
2. **English Sea Captain Placement**: Place sea captains as naval leaders on English naval units in home ports
3. **Holy Roman Religious Preference**: First turn of 6-player game only — secretly record preference
4. **Place Arriving Leaders**: Army leaders → any fortified friendly home space. Naval leaders → any friendly home port. Protestant may hold leaders on power card for later rebellion. Don John may switch army/naval type.
5. **Choose Home Card**: Discard 1 of 2 Home cards face-down (Exception: Ottoman under Suleiman keeps both). Activated major powers discard all Home cards.
6. **Move Formation from Capital**: One formation, free (0 CP). Restrictions:
   - Only units starting in capital
   - Path of any length, all spaces friendly-controlled, no unrest
   - May cross exactly 1 sea zone (friendly port to friendly port)
   - Max 5 units (+leaders) across sea zone
   - Cannot cross a pass
   - Cannot enter space with non-friendly units
   - Cannot enter space with enemy units
   - No naval units of other major powers in sea zones crossed (unless loaned)
   - Spain/Protestant: from either capital (not both)

## 11. ACTION PHASE (Section 11)

Powers take impulses in Impulse Order. Each impulse:

### Three options to start an impulse:
1. **Play card for Command Points (CP)**: CP = shield number (upper-left). Spend on actions from power card. Card to discard pile.
2. **Play card as Event**: Execute printed effects. Card to discard pile (or remove from game if specified).
3. **Pass**: Voluntary if hand ≤ admin rating and no Home/Mandatory cards unplayed. Mandatory if no cards in hand. Can resume later — passing doesn't lock you out.

**Cannot pass if:**
- Home card not yet played
- Mandatory Event in hand unplayed
- More cards in hand than ruler's Administrative Rating

**Action Phase ends** when all 6 powers pass in consecutive impulses.

### Extending with a Treasure
- After playing a card (CP or event), may also play a treasure
- Three auto-resolve treasures: Draw Card, Science Bonus, 1 VP
- CP treasures (1–5 CP): Must choose different action types than those from the card played for CP. No restriction if card was an event. After Mandatory Event, actions must differ from the 2 CP actions.

### 11.1 Actions
- Cost 1–5 CP each
- Actions executed one at a time, completed before spending remaining CP
- CP cannot accumulate between impulses
- Same action may be repeated back-to-back within impulse
- **Exception**: Patronize Artist and Patronize Scientist — max once per turn per power
- Action types are color-coded on power cards (brown = land, blue = naval, etc.)

## 12. CONTROL AND UNREST (Section 12)

### 12.1 Line of Communication (LOC)
Required for Assault and Control Unfortified Space actions.
- Trace path from friendly-controlled fortified home space (yours or ally's, including minor power allies)
- All spaces on path (except endpoint): friendly-controlled, free of enemy units (including naval/leaders), free of unrest
- Sea zones may be part of LOC if each contains a friendly naval unit
- Path connects to sea zones through friendly port (endpoint can be non-friendly port)

### 12.2 Unfortified Space Control
- **Cost**: 1 CP (Control Unfortified Space action)
- **Requirements**:
  - Space is independent or enemy-controlled
  - Space is unfortified
  - Active power has LOC to the space
  - Either: (a) active power's units occupy the space, or (b) active power's units adjacent AND no enemy units adjacent (pass does NOT count as adjacent for this)
  - Space not occupied by non-allied land units
- Place new control marker; maintain religious influence side
- Naval units in port must retreat to adjacent sea zone

### 12.3 Fortified Space Control
- Changes only via: Negotiation, Siege, Rebellion, or Event

### 12.4 Unrest
- Placed by conversion attempts and events
- Four events (Homeland Uprising, Morisco Revolt, Puritans, Rising of the North): must be placed in **unoccupied** spaces
- **Effects of Unrest**:
  - No spring deployment, retreat, or avoid battle into/through
  - No LOC through
  - No unit construction in
  - Blocks adjacent conversion attempts if only adjacent Protestant/Catholic source is in unrest
  - Not counted as Protestant for VP
  - Key in unrest: Place Unrest marker on power card box — reduces VP and cards
  - Fortress in unrest: No die against adjacent piracy
- **Removing Unrest**: 1 CP (Control Unfortified Space action). No LOC required. Requires: (a) own units occupy, or (b) own units adjacent and no enemy adjacent (pass not adjacent)

### 12.5 Displacement
- When control changes via negotiation/intervention/rebellion/event
- Land units/army leaders → nearest friendly fortified space or capital
- Naval units/leaders → nearest friendly port
- Count sea zones as 1 space. Ties: owning player chooses.

## 13. MOVEMENT (Section 13)

### 13.1 Land Movement Procedure
- **Move Formation In Clear**: 1 CP
- **Move Formation Over Pass**: 2 CP

**Land Movement Procedure:**
1. Declare Formation (must start in same space, legal formation)
2. Declare Destination Space (must be adjacent)
3. Expend CP (1 or 2)
4. Play Response Cards (Storms)
5. Conduct Interceptions
6. Conduct Avoid Battle
7. Withdraw into Fortification (if ≤4 enemy units after intercept/avoid)
8. Fight Field Battle (if enemy units still present outside fortifications)

**Restrictions:**
- All units must start in same space, legal formation
- May enter: own spaces, independent spaces, spaces of at-war power, spaces of ally
- May NOT enter space with units from another power unless: all allies, all enemies (with specific siege exceptions), or siege relief scenarios
- Cannot move if lost field battle earlier in impulse
- Cannot move if besieging a space put under siege earlier in impulse
- Leaders alone: cannot enter enemy-controlled or enemy-occupied spaces. Lone leader in unfortified space when enemy enters → captured. Lone naval leader → Turn Track.

### 13.2 Interception
- Enemy stack adjacent to destination may attempt intercept
- Multiple stacks: resolved one at a time, in Impulse Order
- Once one power succeeds, no other power may intercept (but successful power may intercept from additional spaces)

**Interception Procedure:**
1. Declare Formation
2. Roll 2d6 + best Battle Rating of leader. Ottoman +1 if cavalry present. Non-Ottoman −1 if target has Ottoman cavalry. +1 for Telescope bonus. **Success: 9+**
3. If successful: place in destination, treated as present before moving force
4. Repeat for other formations from different spaces
5. If any intercept succeeded → field battle (interceptors cannot avoid/withdraw)

**Restrictions:**
- Cannot intercept formations that are avoiding battle, intercepting, or retreating
- Cannot intercept across a pass
- Only at-war powers
- Units that already tried intercepting this impulse cannot try again
- Units under siege cannot intercept
- Leaders cannot intercept alone (unless friendly units in destination)
- Cannot intercept formation moving into friendly unbesieged fortified space

### 13.3 Avoid Battle
- Enemy units in destination space may try to move to adjacent space
- Multiple allied powers: each resolves in Impulse Order
- **Always optional**

**Avoid Battle Procedure:**
1. Declare Attempts
2. Designate Destination (must meet restrictions)
3. Choose Units (can exceed formation limit; can leave some behind)
4. Roll 2d6 + best Battle Rating. Ottoman +1 cavalry. Non-Ottoman −1 vs Ottoman cavalry. +1 Telescope. **Success: 9+**
   - **Exception**: If every unit already lost a field battle this impulse → automatic success (no roll)
5. If successful: place in chosen adjacent space

**Restrictions:**
- Not into independent/non-allied spaces
- Not into spaces in unrest or containing enemy units
- Not into sea zones
- Not into space vacated by the attacking formation
- Not if under siege
- Not if own units intercepted into this space this Move action
- Leader alone cannot avoid battle

### 13.4 Withdrawing into Fortifications
Conditions (all must be met):
- Destination space is fortified
- Enemy power (or ally) controls the space
- ≤4 units (plus any leaders) present after intercept/avoid resolution

- Always optional. If withdrawn, all units must go together.
- If active formation has more units → siege (Section 15)
- If active formation does not have more:
  1. If CP remain: spend 1 CP (2 over pass) to continue to adjacent space
  2. Otherwise: retreat back to starting space (free, but if later forced back → eliminated)

## 14. FIELD BATTLE (Section 14)

**Field Battle Procedure:**
1. **Play Response Cards**: Both sides (attacker first) may play Reiters. Excess attacker units beyond formation limit don't contribute dice.
2. **Attacker Dice**: 1 die per land unit + Battle Rating of best leader
3. **Defender Dice**: 1 die per land unit + Battle Rating of best leader + **1 die for being defender**
4. **Attacker Combat Cards**
5. **Defender Combat Cards** (each defending power may play)
6. **Roll Dice**: 5 or 6 = hit
7. **Declare Winner**: Most hits wins. **Tie → defender wins.**
8. **Take Casualties**: 1 unit per hit. If both eliminated: side with more dice retains 1 unit. Same dice count: defender retains 1.
9. **Capture Leaders**: Eliminated side's leaders captured (placed on enemy power card)
10. **Retreat**: Losing side retreats (Section 14.1)
11. **Check for Siege**: If in fortified space and active player won

### 14.1 Retreat
- Losing power retreats to single adjacent space
- In fortified space: up to 4 units may withdraw into fortifications; rest retreat
- **Restrictions**: Not into unrest, not into enemy-occupied, not into sea zone, not into independent/non-allied space
- Defender: cannot retreat into space attacker came from
- Attacker: must retreat to space they came from
- No legal retreat space → eliminated; leaders captured

## 15. SIEGE (Section 15)

Siege begins when besieging stack outnumbers units inside fortification.

### 15.1 Assault
- **Cost**: 1 CP (Assault/Foreign War action)
- Cannot assault in same impulse siege was initiated
- Max 1 assault per space per impulse

**Requirements:**
- Units placed space under siege in previous impulse
- Assaulting power has LOC
- Controlling power has no naval unit in adjacent sea zone (or assaulter has more naval units adjacent). Corsairs ignored for blockade.

**Assault Procedure:**
1. Declare Formation
2. Response Cards (Storms)
3. **Attacker Dice (no defending units)**: 1 die per land unit (cavalry ignored) + leader Battle Rating
4. **Attacker Dice (with defenders)**: 1 die per 2 land units (cavalry ignored, round up) + leader Battle Rating
5. **Defender Dice**: 1 die per defending land unit (cavalry ignored) + leader Battle Rating + **1 die for being defender** (2 if Ravelin science bonus)
6. Attacker Combat Cards
7. Defender Combat Cards
8. Roll Dice: 5 or 6 = hit
9. **Successful Assault**: Attacker scored ≥1 hit AND no defending land units remain AND ≥1 attacking unit survived. Attacker gains control. Besieged leaders captured. Response card Sack!/Spanish Fury may be played.
10. **Unsuccessful Assault**: Otherwise. Siege continues if besiegers still outnumber. If not, attackers must retreat per 15.3.

### 15.2 Relief Forces
- Formation friendly to besieged units enters and initiates field battle
- Units inside fortification (same major power as relief force) may join battle (can exceed formation limit)
- Three outcomes:
  - Active player wins → siege broken, besiegers retreat
  - Active player loses, equal hits → may retreat some units into fortifications (max 4 inside)
  - Active player loses, fewer hits → units inside may retreat back in; relief force retreats

### 15.3 Breaking a Siege
- Siege breaks immediately if besiegers no longer outnumber besieged (cavalry counts)
- Besieging stack must retreat to adjacent space
- **Restrictions**: Not into unrest, not into space with non-allied units, not into sea zone, not into independent/non-allied space
- No legal retreat → eliminated; leaders captured

## 16. NAVAL AFFAIRS (Section 16)

### 16.1 Naval Movement
- **Cost**: 1 CP (Naval Move action)
- ALL naval units of the power (and allies/lent fleets) may move with one Naval Move action
- Each unit moves to 1 adjacent location
- **Orientation**: Units entering sea zone with no friendly units → placed vertically (rotated 90°). After avoid battle resolved → rotate all to normal.

**Naval Movement Procedure:**
1. Declare all naval moves and destinations
2. Execute simultaneously. Place in destination (port = horizontal; sea zone with friends = match orientation; sea zone no friends = vertical). Ocean zone arrivals: Navigation Check if crossing hazardous passage or against trade wind. Roll 2d6 + Navigation rating (+1 Map Projection bonus). Consult Navigation Table:
   - Eliminated: Captain removed from game, colony/loot lost
   - Damaged: Flip to damaged side (if already damaged → remove, loot/colony lost)
   - Survived: May place colony in unoccupied gray settlement
3. Response Cards (Storms)
4. Interceptions (sea zones only). Roll 2d6 + naval leader Battle Rating. −1 if galleon/race-built/Armada in Mediterranean. +1 Telescope. **Success: 9+.** Successful interceptors oriented vertically.
5. Avoid Battle (sea zones only). Conditions: all enemy normal orientation, active power vertical. Roll 2d6 + Battle Rating. Same modifiers. **Success: 9+.**
6. Rotate all vertical units to normal
7. Naval Combat if active power and enemy in same sea zone/port

**Restrictions:**
- Always move to adjacent location
- Cannot move to ports controlled by another player unless enemy naval units present
- Naval leaders must stay with same-power naval unit whenever possible
- Cannot move if lost naval combat earlier in impulse

### 16.2 Naval Combat

**Naval Combat Dice:**
| Unit Type | Dice |
|---|---|
| Corsair or galley (in Atlantic) | 1 die each |
| Galleon, race-built galleon, or galley (in port/Mediterranean) | 2 dice each |
| Spanish Armada | 2, 4, 6, or 8 dice (by strength) |
| Naval leader | Battle Rating in dice |
| Defender in port | +1 die |

**Naval Combat Procedure:**
1. Attacker adds up dice
2. Defender adds up dice
3. Attacker Combat Cards
4. Defender Combat Cards
5. Roll Dice: 5 or 6 = hit
6. Response Cards (Galleasses — Mediterranean only)
7. **Winner**: Most hits. **Tie → defender wins.**
8. **Casualties**: 1 galley/galleon/race-built eliminated per 2 hits. Player chooses which. Race-built galleons in Atlantic: England ignores one hit of "5" per race-built (for casualties only, not winner determination). Ottoman: after galleys, remaining hits eliminate corsairs 1:1. Odd remaining hit vs loser: eliminates 1 extra unit. Odd remaining hit vs winner: ignored. Both eliminated: more dice → retain 1; same dice → defender retains 1.
9. **Retreat**: Port combat → attacker retreats to connected sea zone (regardless of who won). Sea zone → loser retreats to adjacent friendly port or sea zone (both free of enemy).
10. Eliminated naval leaders/units → next turn of Turn Track (Exception: Dry Dock bonus → immediate force pool return)

### 16.3 Naval Transport
- Special Move Formation In Clear action
- **Procedure**:
  1. 1 CP: Formation of ≤5 units (+leaders) from port to adjacent sea zone with own naval units. No interception at sea. Storms may disrupt.
  2. 1 CP each additional sea zone move (must have naval unit in each). Must be able to reach port before impulse ends.
  3. End at port adjacent to last sea zone. Port must have no enemy naval units and be enterable by land movement rules.
  4. Treat end port as Move action (intercept, avoid battle, withdraw, field battle possible)
- Formation under siege cannot use naval transport
- Lost field battle at end port → all transported units eliminated, leaders captured

### 16.4 Piracy on Europe Map
- **Cost**: 2 CP (Initiate Piracy)
- Target: enemy power with port adjacent to sea zone (Ottoman corsairs: can target non-enemy active major powers)
- Spain cannot pirate from zone with Armada
- Max 1 piracy per sea zone per power per turn; max 2 powers per zone per turn; max 3 piracy actions per power per turn

**Europe Piracy Procedure:**
1. Declare piracy, specify target power
2. Target rolls defense dice: 2 per own unit in zone (corsair piracy only) + 1 per non-corsair/non-armada unit in adjacent port/zone (at war with pirate) + 1 per fortress adjacent (not in unrest/siege) + 1 if Ottoman pirating and Knights of St. John adjacent
3. Roll: 5/6 = hit. Hits applied to pirate: (a) eliminate 1 corsair per hit, (b) remaining hits: 1 naval unit per 2 hits, (c) 1 odd hit = 1 extra naval unit
4. If pirates survive: base 1 die (if 1 unit or 1 target port) or 2 dice (otherwise) + leader's Piracy rating
5. Roll piracy dice: 5/6 = hit. Each hit → target must choose:
   - (a) Eliminate own naval unit in/adjacent to zone
   - (b) Pirate draws 1 random card from target's hand
   - (c) Award pirate 1 VP for Piracy
   - (d) Downgrade Spanish Armada 1 level (if applicable)
   - Must divide hits equally among valid awards

### 16.5 Piracy on World Map
- **Cost**: 2 CP. Target Spain or Portugal (no war needed).
- Max same as Europe (1/zone/power/turn, 2 powers/zone/turn, 3 actions/power/turn)
- Restriction: England/France cannot pirate Portugal if allied with Portugal

**World Map Piracy Procedure:**
1. Declare piracy, specify target
2. Defense: Portugal = 1 die per settlement adjacent. Spain = 1 die per patrol + 1 die per fortress in connected settlement (if no patrols/fortresses → 0 dice).
3. Roll defense: 5/6 = hit. 2 hits kill captain. 1 hit on undamaged → damaged. 1 hit on already damaged → eliminated.
4. Pirate dice: Base 1 (damaged or 1 settlement) or 2 + Piracy rating
5. Roll: 5/6 = hit. Each hit → Spain chooses:
   - (a) Eliminate patrol
   - (b) Target Spain: random draw from Spanish treasures (Spain may withhold 1 if fortress present). Target Portugal: random Portuguese treasure.
   - (c) Award pirate 1 VP
   - Divide hits equally among valid awards
6. Store loot: Undamaged = 2 treasures max; damaged = 1
7. Possible return home (if path without hazardous passage to entry zone)

### 16.6 Circumnavigation
- **2 VP** for first circumnavigation; **1 VP** for second
- Conditions vary by power:
  - Ottoman: Indian Ocean → South Pacific → ... → Guinea Coast → Indian Ocean (or reverse)
  - Others: South Atlantic → Chilean Coast → ... → Indian Ocean → Guinea Coast → South Atlantic (or reverse)
- Immediately award VP, return loot, place captain on Turn Track

### 16.7 Antwerp and Rotterdam (Inland Ports)
- No naval units may ever enter
- Naval transport: Can move directly to/from North Sea for 1 CP if connected port (Flushing/Brielle) also controlled
- Assault: Cannot assault if besieged power controls connected port (Exception: assaulter has naval units in North Sea, or Treachery/Siege Engineering played)
- Piracy: Can target if pirate controls connected port
- Religion: Always connected to North Sea for conversion purposes

## 18. RELIGIOUS STRUGGLES (Section 18)

### 18.1 Basic Principles
- Religious influence tracked independently from political control
- **Core Presence Rule**: The final Catholic space or final Protestant space in any religious struggle area can NEVER be removed by any action or event. If it would be flipped, that flip is dropped. Actions/events cannot be initiated if no spaces could be flipped due to this rule.

**Unit Religious Affiliations:**
- **Protestant**: English, Dutch, Huguenot, Scottish regulars/naval
- **Catholic**: Spanish, French, Irish, Papal, Portuguese, Venetian, Walloon regulars/naval
- **Unaligned**: Ottoman, Holy Roman regulars/cavalry/naval; all mercenaries

### 18.2 Preaching Sermons
- **Cost**: 2 CP
- **Spain**: 5 Catholic Conversion attempts in England
- **Protestant**: 5 Protestant Conversion attempts in France, Netherlands, OR Scotland (one area only)

### 18.3 Suppress Heresy
- **Cost**: 2 CP
- **Spain**: 5 Catholic Conversion attempts in Netherlands
- **England**: 5 Protestant Conversion attempts in England
- **France**: 5 Catholic Conversion attempts in France OR Scotland (one area only)

### 18.4 Rebellions
- **Cost**: 2–5 CP (Protestant). More CP = more dice, more spaces, more regulars.
- Target: France or Netherlands (not Scotland)
- Not if allied with France (for France rebellions)
- One rebellion per area per impulse

### 18.5 Religious Procedures

#### Protestant Conversion Attempt Procedure:
1. **Roll dice**: 1 per attempt. (Calvinist Zeal, Belgic Confession, Huguenot Lent: first die auto-"6")
2. **Adjust**: Belgic Confession: 3→4. Iconoclastic Fury: 5→6.
3. **Drop Misses**: 2 and 3 are misses.
4. **Unrest**: If any "1" rolled → take 1 Unrest marker (Iconoclastic Fury: 1 per "1"). May nullify 1 Unrest by reducing a "6" to "5".
5. **Major Conversions (6s)**: Each may either: save for Step 6 (to convert space under Catholic unit/Jesuit), OR flip a space meeting all 3 Protestant Conversion Conditions:
   - (a) In targeted area
   - (b) Adjacent to existing Protestant space (not in unrest, Protestant at start of this step) — including across pass or via port-to-port through sea zone
   - (c) Target does not contain Catholic unit or Jesuit
6. **Remaining Conversions**: "4" and "5" = minor conversion (must meet all 3 conditions). Saved "6" = major conversion (ignores condition c — can flip spaces with Catholic units/Jesuits).
7. **Add Unrest**: Place taken Unrest markers on converted spaces.

#### Catholic Conversion Attempt Procedure:
1. Roll dice (Council of Troubles, Index of Prohibited Books, Tridentine Catechism, English Recusants: first die auto-"6")
2. Drop Misses (2, 3)
3. Unrest: Same as Protestant. May nullify 1 by reducing "6" to "5".
4. Major Conversions (6s): Save for Step 5 (space under Protestant unit) OR flip space meeting all 3 Catholic Conversion Conditions:
   - (a) In targeted area
   - (b) Adjacent to Catholic space (not in unrest, Catholic at start of this step)
   - (c) Target does not contain Protestant unit
5. Remaining Conversions: 4/5 = minor (all 3 conditions). Saved 6 = major (ignores condition c).
6. Add Unrest on converted spaces.

#### Rebellion Procedure:
1. **Specify Target & CP (2–5)**: Target must be in France/Netherlands, under French/Spanish control, Protestant influence, not under siege.
2. **Resolve**: If no Catholic units → auto-success. Otherwise: roll dice = CP spent (+1 die if Protestant home space). 5/6 = hit → eliminate Catholic unit. If any remain → rebellion fails.
3. **Displace**: Leaders and non-Catholic units in target displaced.
4. **Control Spaces**: Target + adjacent qualifying spaces switch to Protestant control. Total flipped ≤ CP spent.
5. **Receive Regulars**: France: 1 Huguenot per 2 full CP. Netherlands: 1 Dutch per 2 full CP.
6. **Update War State**: Protestant now at war with France/Spain if not already.
7. **Add Protestant Capital**: If no capital for this area, place one in Protestant-controlled fortified home space.
8. **Add Military Leader**: Protestant leader for this area enters at target space.

## 25. VICTORY (Section 25)

### 25.1 Automatic Victories (Immediate, during Action Phase)

**Military Victory:**
- Power controls enough keys that Auto Win space on power card is uncovered (no Unrest)
- Not if revealed during Diplomacy Phase and then covered before Action Phase

**Religious Victory:**
- **Protestant Religious Victory**: Protestant influence over **50 spaces** (none in unrest)
- **Catholic Religious Victory**: Protestant spaces drop to **9 or fewer**
  - If ≤3 Protestant spaces in France and Scotland combined → **France wins**
  - Otherwise → **Spain wins**

**Catholic Rebellion Victory:**
- Spain scores **4 more hits than England** in English Catholic Rebellion → Spain wins immediately

If two automatic victories from same action: most VP wins (Standard Victory tie-breaker if tied).

### 25.2 Victory Points

**VP = Base + Special + Bonus**

**Base VP**: From keys controlled. Last uncovered box on power card. Keys in unrest don't count. All covered = 0. Protestant: lower row VP + 3 VP per Netherlands key.

**Special VP:**
- **Ottoman**: None
- **Spain**: From Protestant Spaces track (Spain column)
- **England**: From Protestant Spaces track (England column)
- **France**: Valois Wedding (1 VP after each of 5 Valois royals marries)
- **Holy Roman**: From Protestant Spaces track (Holy Roman column) — secret until revealed
- **Protestant**: From Protestant Spaces track (Protestant column)

**Protestant Spaces**: Tracked on Religious Struggle Card. Spaces in unrest excluded.

**Bonus VP** (awarded during game):

### 25.3 Victory Determination Phase

1. **Paris VP**: France/Spain/Protestant earn VP if controlling Paris (not in unrest):
   - **2 VP**: Turn 5/6/7 + specific leader in Paris (France: Charles IX/Henry III; Spain: Duke of Guise; Protestant: Henry Navarre)
   - **1 VP**: Control without above conditions

2. **Central Europe VP**: +1 VP to Holy Roman if Prague + Vienna + Buda controlled and unrest-free

3. **Standard Victory**: Any power ≥ **25 VP** → game ends. Highest VP wins. Tie: highest VP in previous turn. Still tied: go back further.

4. **Domination Victory**: Power < 25 VP but ≥ **5 VP ahead of every other power**. Only checked **Turn 4+**.

5. **Time Limit Victory**: No winner by end of **Turn 7** → highest VP wins. Tie-breaker same as Standard.
