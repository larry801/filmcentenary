Military victory hotseat verification (2026-11-10, Campaign, 6 players)
=====================================================================

Method: the local hotseat board was opened at /localVQ.  Because a normal
playthrough cannot reach six different Auto Win counts in one session, the
already-loaded boardgame.io master state was cloned and given a near-win set
of keys for one power at a time, then the phase was advanced through the real
flow.  checkVictory/endIf and the rendered board then produced the normal game
over state.  This exercises the same victory check, phase gate and UI path as
a played key capture.  The six Auto Win counts and unrest/Netherlands rules
are also covered by unit and flow tests (militaryVictory.test.ts).
